Cheng Keat Tang

Assistant Professor in Economics
Division of Economics, School of Social Sciences
Nanyang Technological University

My research interests include urban and housing economics, transportation, environmental economics and the economics of crime.

My research has appeared in journals including the Journal of Public Economics, Journal of Urban Economics, Journal of Environmental Economics & Management, Journal of Economic Geography, Energy Economics, Ecological Economics, Land Economics, PLOS ONE, Social Science & Medicine and the Journal of Real Estate Finance and Economics, and has been cited in media including the BBC, Wall Street Journal, Financial Times, Los Angeles Times, The Guardian, Science News and Forbes.

I received my undergraduate and master's degrees from the National University of Singapore, and my PhD from the London School of Economics.

I am happy to supervise undergraduate and graduate students whose research interests coincide with mine.
